Common Questions

Quick answers before you reach out

How quickly do you respond?
We typically respond within 24 hours on business days. For urgent issues, email us directly at info@flyto2.com.
Do you offer custom enterprise solutions?
Yes. We can set up Flyto2 for your team with custom deployment, permission controls, and dedicated support. Contact us to discuss your needs.
Can I request a demo?
Of course. Fill out the contact form above and select 'Sales Inquiry'. We'll schedule a personalized demo for your team.
Where can I report bugs?
You can report bugs on our GitHub repository at github.com/flytohub/flyto-core/issues, or use the contact form above with 'Technical Support' selected.
