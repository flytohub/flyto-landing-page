Flyto2 is an automation tool that operates your computer like a human. Open browsers, click buttons, fill forms, upload files. You do it once, Flyto2 repeats it forever.

You know the process. You know it shouldn't take human effort.

What Makes Flyto2 Different?

Most automation tools only have two states: not running, or finished.

Flyto2 adds a crucial ability:

"Before, during, and when errors occur — you can always pause and verify."

With Flyto2, you can:

  • Run 1 item first to check if it's correct
  • Then run 20 items to validate
  • Once confirmed, run everything

If something goes wrong:

  • Stop immediately
  • Roll back if needed
  • Resume from where it stopped

Automation doesn't have to be "all or nothing."
