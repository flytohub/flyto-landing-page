Flyto2の基本について

What is Flyto2?

Flyto2 is an automation tool that operates your computer like a human. Instead of writing code or learning complicated tools, you simply record your actions and Flyto2 learns to repeat them for you. It's designed to handle the tedious, repetitive tasks that steal your time - copying data, filling forms, moving files between apps - so you can focus on work that actually matters.

Do I need to know how to code?

No coding required! Flyto2 is designed for everyone, not just developers. If you can click and type, you can use Flyto2. Simply record yourself doing a task once, and Flyto2 learns to repeat it. There's no programming, no scripting, no technical setup - just show and go.

How is Flyto2 different from other automation tools?

Unlike other tools, Flyto2 lets you pause, verify, and resume at any step. You always stay in control - check what it's about to do, correct mistakes before they happen, and resume exactly where you left off. Plus, Flyto2 can work completely offline, keeping your data private and secure on your own device.

Is my data safe with Flyto2?

Absolutely. Flyto2 takes a safety-first approach. You can run it completely offline - no cloud, no external servers, just your machine. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to use cloud features. We believe automation should help you, not expose you to risks.

はじめに

Flyto2の始め方

What devices does Flyto2 work on?

Flyto2 works on Mac, Windows, and Linux computers. It's designed to be lightweight, so even older machines can run it smoothly. For most tasks, you just need a regular computer - no special hardware required.

How do I record my first task?

Just four simple steps: (1) Click "Record" to start capturing your actions, (2) Do the task you want to automate - click, type, copy, paste as you normally would, (3) Click "Stop" when you're done, (4) Give your recording a name and save it. That's it! Flyto2 learned what you did and can repeat it anytime.

Can Flyto2 work with any application?

Flyto2 works with websites, desktop applications, and file operations. It can interact with your browser, spreadsheets, documents, email, and most programs on your computer. If you can click it or type in it, Flyto2 can probably automate it.

Can I schedule tasks to run automatically?

Yes! You can schedule any recorded task to run at specific times - daily, weekly, or at custom intervals. Set it up once and let Flyto2 handle the rest. You can also trigger tasks based on events, like when a file appears in a folder.

ライセンスと料金

プラン、料金、ライセンス管理について

What's included in the free plan?

The free plan lets you try Flyto2 with basic recording and playback features. It's perfect for getting started and testing if Flyto2 works for your needs. You can record tasks, play them back, and experience the core functionality before deciding if you want more.

What's the difference between Cloud and Offline licenses?

Cloud plans are monthly subscriptions with automatic updates and sync across devices. Offline licenses are one-time annual purchases that work completely without internet - perfect for businesses that need strict data privacy, work in secure environments, or simply prefer to keep everything local.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes! Start with the free plan, and upgrade whenever you're ready. You can switch between plans at any time. When you upgrade, you get instant access to new features. There's no lock-in - you're in control.

Do you offer discounts?

Yes! We offer discounts for students, educators, non-profits, and teams. Contact us at sales@flyto2.com to learn about available programs and pricing options for your situation.
