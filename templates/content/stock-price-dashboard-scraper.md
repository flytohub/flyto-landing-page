## How It Works

The Stock Price Dashboard Scraper template pulls real-time stock prices, daily changes, volume, and market cap data from financial websites. It visits your chosen stock tickers, extracts the latest market data, and saves everything as structured JSON for tracking and analysis.

Individual investors and finance teams need quick access to market data without paying for expensive API subscriptions. This template delivers the same data by reading it directly from public financial pages.

### Use Cases

- **Portfolio tracking** — Monitor your stock holdings with automated daily price checks and export historical data for performance analysis
- **Market research** — Collect stock data across sectors to identify trends, compare performance, and spot opportunities
- **Financial reporting** — Automate data collection for weekly or monthly investment reports and dashboards
- **Trading signals** — Track price movements and volume changes as inputs for your trading strategy

## Getting Started

1. **Import the Stock Price Dashboard Scraper** from the Flyto2 template marketplace
2. **Enter your stock tickers** — provide ticker symbols or links to the financial pages you want to track
3. **Schedule daily runs** — Flyto2 collects prices at market close and builds a historical dataset over time

No API keys or paid data subscriptions required. The template reads publicly available market data from financial websites.
