## How It Works

The LinkedIn Job Listings Scraper template extracts job postings from LinkedIn search results — including job title, company name, location, posting date, and job description. It navigates LinkedIn's job search pages, collects listing details, and exports the data for analysis or tracking.

Job seekers applying to dozens of positions and recruiters sourcing candidates both benefit from having structured, searchable job data instead of scrolling through endless listings manually.

### Use Cases

- **Job seekers** — Collect all relevant job postings for your target role and location into a spreadsheet for organized tracking
- **Recruiters** — Monitor competitor job postings to understand hiring trends, salary ranges, and in-demand skills
- **Market researchers** — Analyze job listing volume and requirements across industries to identify workforce trends
- **Career coaches** — Gather real job descriptions to help clients tailor resumes and identify skill gaps

## Getting Started

1. **Import the LinkedIn Job Listings Scraper** from the Flyto2 template marketplace
2. **Set your search criteria** — enter job title, location, and any filters like experience level or remote work
3. **Run the scraper** — Flyto2 collects listings from the search results and exports them as CSV or JSON

Build a personal job database that you can filter, sort, and track — far more efficient than bookmarking individual LinkedIn pages.
