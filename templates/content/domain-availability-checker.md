## How It Works

The Domain Availability Checker template automates the tedious process of searching for available domain names. Provide a list of domain ideas, and Flyto2 checks each one against registrar websites — reporting which domains are available, taken, or premium-priced.

Finding the right domain name often means testing dozens of variations. This template checks them all at once, so you can focus on picking the best one instead of typing each name manually.

### Use Cases

- **Startup founders** — Check availability for brand name variations across .com, .io, .co, and other TLDs in a single run
- **Domain investors** — Scan bulk lists of keyword-based domains to find undervalued registration opportunities
- **Marketing agencies** — Quickly verify domain options for client campaigns or product launches
- **Brand protection** — Monitor whether similar domain names have been registered by competitors or squatters

## Getting Started

1. **Import the Domain Availability Checker** from the Flyto2 template marketplace
2. **Enter your domain list** — type domain names manually or upload a CSV with your variations
3. **Run the check** — Flyto2 queries each domain and returns availability status with pricing details

Skip the repetitive copy-paste into registrar search bars. Check hundreds of domains in the time it takes to check one manually.
