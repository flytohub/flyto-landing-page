## How It Works

The Bulk URL Shortener template takes a list of long URLs and converts them into short links using popular URL shortening services — automatically. Instead of copying and pasting each link one at a time, Flyto2 processes your entire list in a single run.

Marketing teams and content creators deal with dozens of tracking URLs daily. This template turns a tedious manual task into a hands-off batch operation.

### Use Cases

- **Campaign launches** — Shorten hundreds of UTM-tagged URLs for email campaigns, ads, or social media posts in minutes
- **Affiliate marketers** — Generate clean, trackable short links for product recommendations across multiple platforms
- **Social media managers** — Prepare shortened URLs in bulk for scheduled posts across channels
- **Documentation teams** — Replace long resource links with concise short URLs for guides, wikis, and training materials

## Getting Started

1. **Import the Bulk URL Shortener** template from the Flyto2 marketplace
2. **Upload your URL list** — provide a CSV or paste URLs directly into the workflow input
3. **Run and export** — Flyto2 shortens each URL and outputs the results as a downloadable list with original and short link pairs

No API keys or developer setup required. The template drives the shortening service through the browser, just like you would — but faster.
