## How It Works

The Broken Link Checker template crawls any website and identifies links that return errors — 404 pages, timeouts, or redirect loops. It visits each link on your site, records the HTTP status, and generates a clean report so you can fix issues before they hurt your search rankings.

Search engines penalize sites with broken links, and visitors bounce when they hit dead ends. This template helps you maintain a healthy website without manually clicking through every page.

### Use Cases

- **SEO audits** — Find and fix broken internal and external links that damage your search engine ranking
- **Website migrations** — Verify all links still work after moving to a new domain or restructuring URLs
- **Content teams** — Regularly check blog posts and documentation pages for outdated or removed links
- **Client deliverables** — Run a link health report for clients as part of monthly website maintenance

## Getting Started

1. **Import the Broken Link Checker** from the Flyto2 template marketplace
2. **Enter your website URL** — the template will crawl all pages and check every link it finds
3. **Review the report** — broken links are exported with their status codes and source pages for easy fixing

Schedule weekly runs to catch broken links early, before they affect user experience or SEO performance.
