## How It Works

The Price Drop Alert template monitors product prices on any e-commerce website and notifies you when a price falls below your target threshold. It checks the product page on a schedule, compares the current price to your set limit, and triggers an alert the moment a deal appears.

Instead of checking prices manually every day, let Flyto2 watch for you. Whether it's electronics, clothing, or home goods, you'll know the instant a product hits your budget.

### Use Cases

- **Smart shopping** — Set a target price for items on your wishlist and get alerted only when the price is right
- **Reseller arbitrage** — Monitor supplier prices and act instantly when margins become profitable
- **Holiday deal hunting** — Track products ahead of Black Friday, Prime Day, or seasonal sales to catch flash deals
- **Budget planning** — Watch big-ticket items over weeks and buy when the price dips into your budget range

## Getting Started

1. **Import the Price Drop Alert** from the Flyto2 template marketplace
2. **Set your product and target price** — enter the product page URL and the maximum price you're willing to pay
3. **Enable scheduled monitoring** — Flyto2 checks the price at your chosen interval and sends an alert when it drops

Pair with the Send Telegram Alert template for instant mobile notifications when your target price is reached.
