## How It Works

The Website Screenshot Gallery template captures full-page screenshots of multiple websites in a single run. Provide a list of URLs, and Flyto2 visits each one, takes a high-resolution screenshot, and organizes them into a named gallery you can browse or share.

Designers, agencies, and researchers often need visual snapshots of multiple websites for inspiration boards, competitive analysis, or documentation. This template eliminates the manual screenshot-and-crop workflow entirely.

### Use Cases

- **Design inspiration** — Capture homepage designs from dozens of websites to build mood boards and reference galleries
- **Competitive monitoring** — Take regular screenshots of competitor websites to track design changes, messaging updates, and new features
- **Client presentations** — Create visual comparisons of industry websites to support web design proposals and audits
- **Website archiving** — Maintain a timestamped visual record of how websites look at specific points in time

## Getting Started

1. **Import the Website Screenshot Gallery** from the Flyto2 template marketplace
2. **Enter your URL list** — provide the websites you want to capture, one per line or as a CSV
3. **Run and browse** — Flyto2 visits each site, captures full-page screenshots, and saves them with descriptive filenames

Schedule weekly runs to build a visual timeline of how websites evolve over time — useful for tracking design trends or monitoring brand presence.
