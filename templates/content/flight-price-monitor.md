## How It Works

The Flight Price Monitor template tracks airfare prices on travel booking sites and alerts you when fares change. It navigates to search results for your chosen route and dates, extracts current prices, and logs them over time — helping you book at the lowest possible fare.

Airline prices fluctuate constantly based on demand, time of day, and booking window. This template removes the guesswork by monitoring prices automatically so you never overpay for flights.

### Use Cases

- **Budget travelers** — Watch prices for upcoming trips and book when fares drop to your target price
- **Business travel managers** — Track flight costs for recurring routes to optimize corporate travel budgets
- **Travel agencies** — Monitor fare trends across popular destinations to advise clients on the best booking windows
- **Digital nomads** — Compare prices across multiple departure cities and dates to find the cheapest option

## Getting Started

1. **Import the Flight Price Monitor** from the Flyto2 template marketplace
2. **Set your route** — enter departure city, destination, and travel dates
3. **Schedule daily monitoring** — Flyto2 checks prices at your preferred frequency and logs every change

Combine with the Send Telegram Alert template to get instant notifications on your phone when prices drop below your threshold.
