## How It Works

The Send Telegram Alert template delivers automated notifications to any Telegram chat or group. When paired with other Flyto2 workflows, it sends real-time messages — price alerts, status updates, error notifications, or custom reports — directly to your phone through Telegram.

Telegram bots are one of the fastest ways to receive push notifications. This template connects your browser automations to Telegram so you never miss an important event.

### Use Cases

- **Price monitoring alerts** — Get instant Telegram messages when tracked products drop below your target price
- **Uptime notifications** — Receive alerts when your website goes down or response times spike
- **Task completion reports** — Get notified when long-running automation workflows finish processing
- **Team updates** — Push automated status updates to a shared Telegram group for your team

## Getting Started

1. **Import the Send Telegram Alert** from the Flyto2 template marketplace
2. **Connect your Telegram bot** — enter your bot token and chat ID (the template includes setup instructions)
3. **Configure the message** — define the alert text or connect it as the final step in another Flyto2 workflow

Chain this template after any monitoring workflow — price trackers, uptime monitors, or data scrapers — to get instant mobile notifications when something important happens.
