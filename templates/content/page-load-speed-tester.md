## How It Works

The Page Load Speed Tester template measures how fast your web pages load by navigating to each URL and recording key performance metrics — total load time, time to first byte, and fully rendered time. It tests multiple pages in sequence and exports the results for comparison.

Page speed directly affects user experience and search engine rankings. This template gives you a simple way to benchmark your site's performance without setting up complex monitoring tools.

### Use Cases

- **Website optimization** — Identify the slowest pages on your site and prioritize performance improvements
- **Pre-launch testing** — Verify that new pages and features meet your load time targets before going live
- **Competitive benchmarking** — Compare your site's speed against competitor websites to understand where you stand
- **Ongoing monitoring** — Schedule regular speed tests to detect performance regressions after deployments or content updates

## Getting Started

1. **Import the Page Load Speed Tester** from the Flyto2 template marketplace
2. **Enter your URLs** — add the pages you want to test, or provide a sitemap URL for full-site testing
3. **Run and analyze** — Flyto2 loads each page, captures timing metrics, and exports results for review

Schedule weekly speed tests to track performance trends and catch slowdowns early, before they affect your users or search rankings.
