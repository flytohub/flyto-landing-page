## How It Works

The YouTube Channel Stats Tracker template collects key metrics from any YouTube channel — subscriber count, total views, video count, and recent upload performance. It visits the channel page, extracts the latest numbers, and logs them over time so you can track growth trends.

Content creators, marketers, and media companies need consistent visibility into YouTube channel performance. This template automates the data collection without requiring YouTube API keys or developer credentials.

### Use Cases

- **Content creators** — Track your own channel growth with daily subscriber and view counts to measure the impact of new content
- **Brand partnerships** — Evaluate potential sponsorship or collaboration partners by monitoring their channel metrics over time
- **Competitive analysis** — Compare subscriber growth and upload frequency across competing channels in your niche
- **Agency reporting** — Automate weekly or monthly YouTube performance reports for clients with historical trend data

## Getting Started

1. **Import the YouTube Channel Stats Tracker** from the Flyto2 template marketplace
2. **Enter channel URLs** — provide one or more YouTube channel links to monitor
3. **Schedule tracking** — run daily or weekly to build a growth dataset and spot performance trends

Export the data as CSV or JSON to create charts, dashboards, or reports that show channel performance over weeks and months.
