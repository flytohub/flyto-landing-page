## How It Works

The Invoice PDF Downloader template automates the process of downloading invoices from web portals. It logs into your billing dashboard, navigates to the invoices section, and downloads each invoice as a PDF file — saving you from clicking through pages of billing history manually.

Accounting teams and freelancers spend hours each month collecting invoices from SaaS tools, hosting providers, and service platforms. This template handles the entire download process in one run.

### Use Cases

- **Monthly bookkeeping** — Download all invoices from SaaS platforms like AWS, Stripe, or Google Workspace at the end of each billing cycle
- **Tax preparation** — Collect a full year of invoices from multiple vendors in one batch for tax filing
- **Expense reporting** — Automatically gather receipts and invoices needed for corporate expense reimbursement
- **Audit readiness** — Maintain a complete archive of PDF invoices organized by date and vendor

## Getting Started

1. **Import the Invoice PDF Downloader** from the Flyto2 template marketplace
2. **Configure the portal** — set the billing portal URL and login credentials
3. **Run the download** — Flyto2 logs in, finds all available invoices, and saves each one as a named PDF file

Schedule monthly runs right after billing cycles to keep your invoice archive up to date without lifting a finger.
