## How It Works

The Website Uptime Monitor template checks whether your websites are online and responding correctly. It visits each URL on a schedule, verifies the page loads successfully, measures response time, and logs any downtime or errors it encounters.

Downtime costs businesses revenue and trust. This template provides a simple, no-setup monitoring solution that alerts you the moment your site goes down — without monthly subscriptions to third-party monitoring services.

### Use Cases

- **Small business owners** — Monitor your company website and online store for outages without paying for enterprise monitoring tools
- **Freelance developers** — Keep tabs on client websites you've built and get notified before clients notice problems
- **SaaS operators** — Track the availability of your web application, API endpoints, and landing pages around the clock
- **Agency teams** — Monitor all client websites from a single workflow and generate uptime reports for monthly reviews

## Getting Started

1. **Import the Website Uptime Monitor** from the Flyto2 template marketplace
2. **Add your URLs** — enter the websites you want to monitor for uptime and response time
3. **Set a schedule** — run every 5, 15, or 60 minutes depending on how critical your uptime requirements are

Pair with the Send Telegram Alert or Weather Forecast to Slack template to receive instant notifications when a site goes down.
