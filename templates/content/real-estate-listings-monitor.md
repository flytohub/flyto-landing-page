## How It Works

The Real Estate Listings Monitor template tracks property listings on real estate websites and alerts you to new listings, price changes, and status updates. It scrapes listing details — price, address, square footage, bedrooms, and listing date — and logs changes over time.

In competitive housing markets, being first to see a new listing can make the difference between landing your dream home and missing out. This template gives you an automated edge.

### Use Cases

- **Home buyers** — Get notified immediately when new properties matching your criteria hit the market in your target neighborhoods
- **Real estate investors** — Monitor price reductions across multiple markets to spot undervalued properties
- **Property managers** — Track rental listing prices in your area to stay competitive with market rates
- **Real estate agents** — Watch competitor listings and new inventory to stay informed for client conversations

## Getting Started

1. **Import the Real Estate Listings Monitor** from the Flyto2 template marketplace
2. **Set your search criteria** — enter the real estate website URL with your preferred filters (location, price range, property type)
3. **Schedule monitoring** — run hourly or daily to catch new listings and price changes as they happen

Export results as JSON or CSV to build a historical dataset of property prices in your target market.
