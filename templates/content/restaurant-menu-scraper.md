## How It Works

The Restaurant Menu Scraper template extracts menu items, prices, and descriptions from restaurant websites and food delivery platforms. It navigates to the menu page, captures every dish with its details, and exports the data as structured JSON or CSV.

Food industry professionals, delivery platforms, and market researchers need structured menu data that's usually locked inside website layouts. This template extracts it cleanly and consistently.

### Use Cases

- **Food delivery startups** — Collect restaurant menu data to populate your platform without manual data entry
- **Market research** — Analyze pricing trends across restaurants in a specific area or cuisine category
- **Competitor analysis** — Track how rival restaurants price similar dishes and update their offerings
- **Nutritional apps** — Gather menu items and descriptions as input for calorie estimation and dietary tracking tools

## Getting Started

1. **Import the Restaurant Menu Scraper** from the Flyto2 template marketplace
2. **Enter the menu URL** — provide the restaurant's website or food delivery page link
3. **Run and export** — Flyto2 extracts all menu items with names, prices, and descriptions into a clean data file

Process multiple restaurants in a single batch by providing a list of URLs. The template handles pagination and multi-page menus automatically.
