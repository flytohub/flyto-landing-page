## How It Works

The Google Search Results Scraper template extracts organic search results for any query — including titles, URLs, descriptions, and ranking positions. It performs the search in a real browser, captures the results page, and exports the data as structured JSON or CSV.

SEO professionals and marketers rely on search result data to inform content strategy and track keyword rankings. This template automates the data collection so you can spend time on analysis instead of manual searching.

### Use Cases

- **SEO rank tracking** — Monitor where your website appears for target keywords and track position changes over time
- **Competitor research** — See which pages rank for keywords in your niche and analyze their content strategies
- **Content gap analysis** — Discover search queries where competitors rank but your site doesn't appear
- **Market research** — Collect search results for product-related keywords to understand customer intent and demand

## Getting Started

1. **Import the Google Search Results Scraper** from the Flyto2 template marketplace
2. **Enter your search queries** — provide one or more keywords to search
3. **Run and export** — Flyto2 performs each search and saves the top results with title, URL, snippet, and position

Schedule weekly runs to build a keyword ranking history and spot trends in your search visibility over time.
