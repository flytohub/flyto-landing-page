## How It Works

The News Headlines Aggregator template collects top headlines from multiple news websites in a single run. It visits each source, extracts article titles, links, publication times, and summaries, then combines everything into one structured feed you can review at a glance.

Staying informed across multiple news sources usually means opening a dozen tabs every morning. This template consolidates that process into an automated workflow that delivers a curated news digest on your schedule.

### Use Cases

- **Media monitoring** — Track headlines from industry-specific publications to stay ahead of trends in your sector
- **PR and communications** — Aggregate news coverage about your brand or competitors across major outlets
- **Research teams** — Collect daily headlines on specific topics for ongoing market or policy research
- **Personal news digest** — Build a custom morning briefing from your favorite news sources without visiting each site

## Getting Started

1. **Import the News Headlines Aggregator** from the Flyto2 template marketplace
2. **Add your news sources** — enter the URLs of news websites or specific section pages you want to monitor
3. **Schedule and review** — run daily to generate a headlines digest exported as JSON or CSV

Combine with the Weather Forecast to Slack or Send Telegram Alert templates to deliver your news digest directly to your team chat.
