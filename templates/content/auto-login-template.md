## How It Works

The Auto Login template saves you from typing credentials over and over again. It securely stores your login details, navigates to any website's sign-in page, fills in your username and password, and completes the authentication flow — all in one click.

This is especially useful when you manage multiple accounts or need to log in to internal tools every morning. Combine it with other Flyto2 templates to build multi-step workflows that start with authentication.

### Use Cases

- **Daily workflow kickoff** — Automatically log in to your CRM, project management, or analytics dashboard each morning
- **Multi-account management** — Switch between client portals or social media accounts without manual credential entry
- **QA and testing** — Verify login flows across staging and production environments repeatedly
- **Chained automation** — Use as the first step before running data extraction or form submission templates

## Getting Started

1. **Import the template** from the Flyto2 marketplace and open it in the editor
2. **Enter your credentials** — provide the login URL, username, and password fields
3. **Run the workflow** — Flyto2 navigates to the page, fills the form, and logs you in automatically

Pair this with scheduled runs to eliminate repetitive logins from your daily routine.
