## How It Works

The Amazon Product Price Tracker template automates price monitoring on Amazon. It navigates to any product page, extracts the current price, and saves the results as structured JSON data — all without writing a single line of code.

Whether you're a reseller tracking competitor prices or a shopper waiting for deals, this template handles the repetitive checking for you.

### Use Cases

- **E-commerce sellers** — Monitor competitor pricing across multiple products daily
- **Deal hunters** — Track price history and get notified when prices drop
- **Market researchers** — Collect pricing data across product categories for analysis
- **Dropshippers** — Automatically check supplier prices before listing updates

## Getting Started

1. **Open Flyto2** and import the Amazon Product Price Tracker template from the marketplace
2. **Set the product URL** — paste any Amazon product page link
3. **Run or schedule** — execute once or set up hourly/daily monitoring

No coding needed. The template handles browser navigation, price extraction, and data export automatically.
