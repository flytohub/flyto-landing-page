## How It Works

The Weather Forecast to Slack template fetches the daily weather forecast from a weather website and posts it to your Slack channel automatically. It extracts temperature, conditions, and precipitation chances, then formats a clean summary and delivers it to your team every morning.

Teams that work outdoors, manage events, or coordinate logistics benefit from having weather data pushed directly into their communication hub — no apps to check, no tabs to open.

### Use Cases

- **Field operations teams** — Get daily weather updates in Slack to plan outdoor work, construction schedules, or delivery routes
- **Event planners** — Receive automated forecasts for event locations so your team can prepare for weather changes
- **Remote team culture** — Share local weather across distributed teams as a lightweight daily icebreaker
- **Agriculture and farming** — Push daily forecasts and precipitation data to channels used for crop management decisions

## Getting Started

1. **Import the Weather Forecast to Slack** from the Flyto2 template marketplace
2. **Set your location and Slack webhook** — enter the city for the forecast and your Slack incoming webhook URL
3. **Schedule a daily run** — Flyto2 fetches the forecast each morning and posts it to your chosen channel

Combine with other data sources to build a comprehensive daily briefing — weather, news headlines, and team metrics all delivered to Slack before your first meeting.
