What you see on the dashboard

Four panels, zero noise. Each one answers a different executive question.

Security Score

A single number on a 250 - 900 scale, mapped to an A-F letter grade. Industry-standard methodology inspired by Bitsight and SecurityScorecard. Your gauge updates after every scan.

Priority Actions

Actionable recommendations ranked by impact. "Fix 20 critical vulnerabilities" beats "upgrade lodash" because the engine weighs blast radius, not just severity. Click any action to jump straight to the findings.

Grade Distribution

A donut chart showing how many repos fall into each letter grade. Spot the outliers instantly: if 22 of 24 repos are A but two are D, you know exactly where to focus.

Security Overview

Critical, high, medium, low -- stacked bar showing the full severity breakdown. 20 Critical + 93 High = 113 findings that need attention before your next release.

How the score works

The score is ratio-based, not count-based. A project with 10,000 assets and 10 issues is fundamentally healthier than one with 10 assets and 10 issues. The engine knows the difference.

A
10,000 assets
10 issues
0.1% issue rate
F
10 assets
10 issues
100% issue rate

Scores are project-type-aware across four dimensions: code quality, security posture, operations readiness, and documentation coverage. Each dimension is weighted by what matters for your stack.

Methodology details available in-app under Dashboard → Scoring Methodology.
