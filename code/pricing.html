Frequently asked questions

Is there really a free tier?

Yes. 3 repos, full scanning, health scoring, and the war room dashboard. Free forever, no credit card required.

Can I try Pro before paying?

Absolutely. 14-day free trial with all Pro features. No credit card required to start.

How do you count repositories?

Each connected GitHub or GitLab repository counts as one repo. Forks of the same repo count separately.

Can I switch plans anytime?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime. Changes take effect immediately and billing is prorated.

Is there per-seat pricing?

No. Pricing is per organization, not per user. Invite your entire team at no extra cost.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major credit cards via Stripe. Enterprise plans can pay by invoice with NET-30 terms.
