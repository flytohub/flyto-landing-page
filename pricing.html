Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch between plans?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your cloud plan at any time. For offline licenses, you can upgrade by paying the difference.
What happens when my offline license expires?
Your workflows continue to work indefinitely. You only lose access to updates and support. Renew anytime to get the latest features.
Do you offer refunds?
Yes, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for all paid plans. No questions asked.
Can I use the free version commercially?
Absolutely! The open source core is MIT licensed. You can use it for any purpose, including commercial projects.
